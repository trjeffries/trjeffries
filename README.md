### Interactive Tableau Projects

__Oh I Loved That Song!:__ A game where you try to guess which of two pre-YouTube number 1 songs has more views on YouTube, with built-in hints, statistics, and a streak counter to keep you going. Alteryx was also used, to scrape the Official Chart Company's website, and many hours were also spent manually searching YouTube, to make sure view counts were taken from the correct video (or, frequently with older songs, videos). Click the image to see how you do!

<div align="center"><a href="https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/tim.jeffries/viz/OhILOVEDThatSong/Dashboard1_1"><img src="/images/oh-i-loved-that-song.png" alt="Oh I LOVED That Song! on Tableau Public"/></a></div>

<br>

__The Mathematical Pub Name Pub Crawl Generator:__ This started out as a project to identify the most unusual pub names in the UK, but developed into a tool to generate a pub crawl, offering a free choice of where to start, and then a choice of either every pub within a mile radius, or if there weren't that many, up to ten pubs out to a twenty mile radius of your previous selection, and then a nice printable map at the end. Additional tools used for this included Python, with the Sentence Transformers framework to identify a pub name's 'Imagination' score, and Figma to generate the beautiful indicators for the ~5% of UK pubs that have a unique name. It's disappointingly slow to render on Tableau Public, largely due to Britain having almost 60,000 pubs, but if you fancy planning an afternoon out, click to try it out!

<a href="https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/tim.jeffries/viz/TheMathematicalPubNamePubCrawlGenerator/Generator"><img src="/images/pub-crawl-generator.png" alt="The Mathematical Pub Name Pub Crawl Generator on Tableau Public"/></a>

<br>

__Night Tube Extension Planner:__ One for anybody who's considering running for Mayor of London, it's more pubs and more spatial data, but this time also housing and urban planning! What would happen if more tube lines were added to the Night Tube map, I found myself idly wondering on a long night bus ride home, and the following was born. Have a play!
